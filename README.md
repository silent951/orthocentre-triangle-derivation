# Orthocentre of a Triangle — Complete Derivation

**By Dhivaan K**

A step-by-step derivation of the orthocentre formula for a triangle formed by the pair of lines
\[
Ax^2+2Hxy+By^2=0
\]
and a third line
\[
Lx+My+N=0.
\]

## Final result

Let
\[
Q=BL^2-2HLM+AM^2.
\]
Then the orthocentre \(K=(h,k)\) is
\[
\boxed{h=-\frac{NL(A+B)}{Q},\qquad k=-\frac{NM(A+B)}{Q}.}
\]

The full derivation, including the intermediate algebra and a verification, is in **[orthocentre-derivation.md](orthocentre-derivation.md)**.

## Assumptions

The derivation uses finite, distinct slopes for the two lines in the homogeneous pair, with \(B\ne0\), \(L+Mm_i\ne0\) for both intersections, and \(Q\ne0\). These conditions avoid vertical-slope and degenerate cases in this slope-based derivation.
